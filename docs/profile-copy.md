### Journalist request answer (Featured / Help a B2B Writer)

Keep to one specific mechanism. Structure:

1. **The direct answer**, first sentence, no preamble
2. **A concrete example** with real mechanics — systems, numbers, what broke
3. **What to do about it**, in one or two steps
4. Sign-off: `Mehrdad Fashami, independent data and revenue operations consultant, mehrdadfashami.com`

Generic advice does not get published. "Data quality matters" loses to "when
your CRM says 500 leads and finance says 40, the fix is definitional, not
investigative — here is the three-column table I build first."
