# Agent Task — Pages & Copy

Work only on branch `agent/pages-copy`.

## Objective
Improve the clarity and usefulness of copy across the Datalift website for small and mid-size non-technical businesses.

## Writing direction
- Make the customer immediately recognize their operational/reporting problem.
- Explain outcomes in simple business language.
- Reduce jargon, vague AI/data language, and unnecessary complexity.
- Make each service/page clearly answer: what problem is this, what do we fix, what changes for the customer, and what should they do next?
- Keep copy concise, human, confident, and practical.
- Ensure CTAs link to the correct internal pages.
- Keep tone and terminology consistent across homepage, services, work/case studies, about, insights, pricing/contact where present.

## Important restriction
Do NOT modify the header, navbar, or hero section. These are owned by other parallel agents.

## QA
Review every routed page for duplicate copy, broken internal links, placeholder text, inconsistent terminology, and overly technical wording. Run build/lint/typecheck after changes.
